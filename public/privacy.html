Privacy Policy

AI Optimizer is designed as a local-first product, and we aim to keep our privacy practices straightforward. We do not sell your personal information, and we only use information needed to operate the product, manage access, and support customers.

Day Place Ltd. ("Day Place", "we", "us", or "our") operates AI Optimizer and related websites, software, and services (collectively, the "Services"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information related to the product, website, and customer communications.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to visitors of the AI Optimizer website, customers using AI Optimizer, and people who contact us about the product.

2. Information we may collect

  • contact information you provide to us, such as your email address;
  • billing, purchase, account, and license information needed to provide access to the product;
  • messages you send us for support or business inquiries;
  • basic website, browser, device, and usage information necessary to operate and improve the service.

Payments may be processed by third-party providers such as Stripe. We do not store full payment card details on our own systems unless explicitly stated.

3. Local-first product behavior

AI Optimizer is designed to run locally on your machine. The product is intended to add a local proxy and caching layer to your workflow rather than route all activity through a remote hosted service controlled by us.

4. How we use information

  • to provide access to AI Optimizer;
  • to validate licenses, trials, or subscriptions;
  • to respond to support requests and product questions;
  • to operate, maintain, and improve the product and website;
  • to comply with legal obligations and protect our systems.

5. Sharing your information

We may share information with trusted third parties that help us operate the business, such as hosting and infrastructure providers, payment processors, email and communications providers, analytics providers, and professional advisers where necessary.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies, analytics tools, and similar technologies to understand website traffic and usage, improve performance and user experience, and maintain security and reliability. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Data retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed to operate the product, manage customer relationships, meet legal obligations, and resolve support or billing issues.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we handle, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date or reflect the revised policy on this page.

11. Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact us at admin@ai-optimizer.org.

We do not sell your personal information.
