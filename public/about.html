About

Local-first AI cost control, built for repeat-heavy real workflows.

AI Optimizer exists because repeated API work gets expensive fast, and most teams do not want to rebuild their whole stack just to control the waste.

Quick answer

AI Optimizer is a local-first desktop app from Day Place that reduces repeated OpenAI and Anthropic API waste by adding a caching proxy on localhost. The goal is practical cost control for developers, agents, scripts, and automations without forcing a whole new platform migration.

What AI Optimizer does

AI Optimizer adds a local caching and control layer in front of your OpenAI or Anthropic workflow. It helps repeated requests resolve locally when the request pattern is stable enough to hit cache, and it gives you visibility into requests and cache hits from one local app.

Why it exists

Repeated AI usage is often more expensive than people expect, especially in scripts, agents, automations, and recurring jobs. AI Optimizer focuses on that practical problem instead of trying to become an all-purpose AI platform.

Why local-first matters

Local-first keeps the control layer close to the workflow. That makes it easier to reason about requests, verify cache behavior, and adopt the product without routing everything through another hosted middle layer.

Who it is for

AI Optimizer is built for developers, agent operators, and technical teams running repeat-heavy tools, scripts, automations, and local assistants where request patterns repeat over time.
